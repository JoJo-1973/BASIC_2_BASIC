    General Electric GE-225

    CPU
    GE-225 (20-bit words)

    With a Datanet-30 communications computer handling the Teletype terminals (Model 33 and 35), the first Dartmouth Time-Sharing System: the first BASIC programs ran on it on 1 May 1964. Replaced by the faster GE-235 around June 1964 (source [5]).
