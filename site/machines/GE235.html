    General Electric GE-235

    Manufacturer
    General Electric
    CPU
    GE-235 (20-bit words: three 6-bit characters)
    Code
    GE235

    The faster GE-225, with the Datanet-30: the Dartmouth Time-Sharing System from mid-1964 to 1967 (Phase I). Floating point of about 30 bits of mantissa (nine to ten digits), binary exponent from -256 to 255 (source [6]). The DTSS-Win simulator [8] emulates this machine. GE sold the GE-235 with the Datanet-30 as the GE-265, with a copy of the Dartmouth system, from early 1965 [9].
