    Casio FX-700P BASIC ›

    CLEAR Direct command

    Erases the program of the current area, or (CLEAR A) the programs of all areas P0-P9.

    Layer
    FX700P_core — Casio FX-700P BASIC
    Dialects
    Casio FX-700P BASIC

    Current area

    CLEAR

    In the manual (FX-700P Instruction Manual, p. 26): CLEAR

    Equivalents in other dialects

    Notes

    The A of the "all areas" form is the letter A, not a keyword: it has no token of its own (the token tables of source [5] have only LIST, CLEAR, SAVE and LOAD).

    In a program: not known on the real machine. In the PokecomGO2 simulator (source [9]) CLEAR in a program clears the variables like VAC (a comment of its author in the source).
