    PRINT Statement

    Types the values of formulas and labels on a line.

    Layer
    DARTMOUTH_4ED_layer — Dartmouth BASIC, 4th edition
    Dialects
    Dartmouth BASIC (4th edition)

    Redefines PRINT of the previous layer.
    Differences: Strings and TAB(n).

    Syntax

    ',' moves to the next of five zones of 15 columns; ';' packs: a number is printed as its sign (minus or space), the digits and one space. A final ',' or ';' keeps the line open.

    PRINT [<label | formula | string | TAB(n)> [<, | ;> <label | formula | string | TAB(n)>]... [, | ;]]

    In the manual (BASIC, 4th Edition (January 1968), p. 34, 38-42): PRINT differs from the rebuilt syntax

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    label M label Text in quotes.
    formula M formula Any formula.
    string M string value A string variable or element.
    n M formula
    truncated to integer    		 Column 0 to 74: integer part, taken modulo 75.

    Notes

    Integers of more than eight digits are printed as 1.34218 E+8; other numbers with up to six significant digits, below 0.1 in E form unless they fit in six decimal places [4] p. 41.

    • Width of the fixed tabulation zones: 15 columns to verify
    • Variable tab stops: No to verify
