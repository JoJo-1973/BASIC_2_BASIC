    SIN Function

    Sine; the argument is in radians.

    Layer
    DARTMOUTH_1ED_core — Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition
    Dialects
    Dartmouth BASIC (1st edition), Dartmouth BASIC (2nd edition), Dartmouth BASIC (3rd edition), Dartmouth BASIC (4th edition), Dartmouth Card BASIC October 1964

    Syntax

    SIN(x)

    In the manual (BASIC Instruction Manual, May 1964 (First Draft), p. 5): SIN(X)

    Returns: Number, range [-1, 1] M

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    x M formula Any formula.
