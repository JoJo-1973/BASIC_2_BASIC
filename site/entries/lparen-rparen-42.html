    ( ) Operator

    Parentheses: the expression inside is evaluated before what surrounds it.

    Layer
    ZX80_4K_core — Sinclair ZX80 BASIC (4K ROM)
    Dialects
    Sinclair ZX80 BASIC 4K ROM

    Syntax

    (e)

    Class: grouping operators

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    e M numeric expression
    or string expression    		 Numeric or string expression.

    Errors

    ErrorWhen
    2 VARIABLE_NOT_FOUND A variable in the expression is not defined. Parameter: e. automatic to verify
    3 SUBSCRIPT_OUT_OF_RANGE An array element in the expression has a subscript out of range. Parameter: e. automatic to verify
    6 OVERFLOW A calculation in the expression goes outside the range from -32768 to 32767. Parameter: e. automatic to verify
