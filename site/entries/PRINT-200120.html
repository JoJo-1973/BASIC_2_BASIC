    PRINT Statement

    Types the values of formulas and labels on a line.

    Layer
    DARTMOUTH_CARD_layer — Card BASIC
    Dialects
    Dartmouth Card BASIC October 1964

    Redefines PRINT of the previous layer.
    Differences: Labels between single quotes; the semicolon is punched as a multiple punch 5-8; 8 numbers per line on the printer.

    Syntax

    ',' moves to the next zone of 15 characters (after the fifth, to the next line); ';' moves to the next multiple of three characters, at least six per number. A PRINT ending with a comma or semicolon does not end the line; PRINT alone skips a line.

    PRINT [<label | formula | label formula> [<, | ;> <label | formula | label formula>]... [, | ;]]

    In the manual (BASIC (1 October 1964), p. 50): PRINT differs from the rebuilt syntax

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    label M label Text in quotes, typed as it is; it fills whole zones.
    formula M formula Any formula: its value is typed.

    Notes

    Single quotes replace the double quotes; the semicolon is a 5-8 multiple punch [2] p. 50. The high-speed printer prints 8 numbers per line, up to 18 in packed format.

    • Width of the fixed tabulation zones: 8 zones per line to verify
    • Variable tab stops: No to verify
