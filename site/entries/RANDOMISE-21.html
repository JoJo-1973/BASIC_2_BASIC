    RANDOMISE Statement

    Seeds the random number generator.

    Layer
    ZX80_4K_core — Sinclair ZX80 BASIC (4K ROM)
    Dialects
    Sinclair ZX80 BASIC 4K ROM

    Syntax

    RANDOMISE [n]

    In the manual (Sinclair ZX80 Operating Manual - A Course in BASIC Programming, p. Appendix II): RANDOMISE n differs from the rebuilt syntax

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    n M numeric expression Seed. Without an argument, or with 0, the video frame counter is used.

    Equivalents in other dialects

    RND_SEED Seed the random numbers

    Errors

    ErrorWhen
    2 VARIABLE_NOT_FOUND A variable in the expression is not defined. Parameter: n. automatic to verify
    3 SUBSCRIPT_OUT_OF_RANGE An array element in the expression has a subscript out of range. Parameter: n. automatic to verify
    6 OVERFLOW A calculation in the expression goes outside the range from -32768 to 32767. Parameter: n. automatic to verify

    Keywords

    KeywordTokenAbbreviation
    RANDOMISE $EF J

    Examples

    Notes

    Sets the seed n of the pseudo-random generator used by RND (the algorithm is in the notes of RND). A negative seed is read as an unsigned 16-bit number: RANDOMISE -5 is the same as a seed of 65531.
