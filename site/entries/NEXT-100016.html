    Casio FX-700P BASIC ›

    NEXT Statement

    Ends a FOR loop: if the variable plus the increment does not pass the end value, the loop is repeated.

    Layer
    FX700P_core — Casio FX-700P BASIC
    Dialects
    Casio FX-700P BASIC

    Syntax

    NEXT v

    In the manual (FX-700P Instruction Manual, p. 45): NEXT v

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    v M numeric variable Variable of the loop.
