    GOSUB Statement

    Calls a subroutine; RETURN goes back to the statement after GOSUB.

    Layer
    FX700P_core — Casio FX-700P BASIC
    Dialects
    Casio FX-700P BASIC

    Line

    GOSUB n

    In the manual (FX-700P Instruction Manual, p. 56): GOSUB {line number | variable} differs from the rebuilt syntax

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    n M numeric expression
    truncated to integer    		 [1, 9999]M
    Range of line numbers: 1-9999 to verify
    missing line: error
    otherwise error 4 ERR4    		 Line number: numeric expression, decimal part cut off; 1 to 9999.

    Program area

    GOSUB #p
    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    p M numeric expression
    truncated to integer    		 [0, 9]M
    		 Program area: numeric expression, decimal part cut off; 0 to 9. Execution goes to the first line of that area.

    Errors

    ErrorWhen
    3 MATHEMATICAL A calculation in the expression goes out of range, or its result is indefinite or impossible. Parameters: n, p. automatic to verify
    4 UNDEFINED_LINE Undefined line number: the line of a GOTO or GOSUB does not exist. Parameter: n. Form: Line. automatic to verify
    6 VARIABLE The expression uses a memory that has not been expanded, or a numeric and a character variable of the same letter. Parameters: n, p. automatic to verify
    7 NESTING More than 8 subroutine levels. to verify
