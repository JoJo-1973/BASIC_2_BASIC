    FOR...TO...STEP Statement

    Starts a loop: the variable goes from the first value to the second in steps of 1 or of the STEP value.

    Layer
    DARTMOUTH_2ED_layer — Dartmouth BASIC, 2nd edition
    Dialects
    Dartmouth BASIC (2nd edition), Dartmouth BASIC (3rd edition), Dartmouth BASIC (4th edition), Dartmouth Card BASIC October 1964

    Redefines FOR...TO...STEP of the previous layer.
    Differences: The loop variable can be any unsubscripted variable (FOR X1).

    Syntax

    FOR v = f1 TO f2 [STEP f3]

    In the manual (BASIC (1 October 1964), p. 29-30, 56): FOR <unsubscripted variable> = <expression> TO <expression> STEP <expression> differs from the rebuilt syntax

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    v M variable An unsubscripted variable: X, X1.
    f1 M formula First value.
    f2 M formula Final value.
    f3 M formula Step; 1 when STEP is omitted; it can be negative or a fraction.

    Equivalents in other dialects
