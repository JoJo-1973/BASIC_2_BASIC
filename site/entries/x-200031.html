    Notes

    A↑B is computed as |A|↑B: for a negative X write X*X*X, not X↑3 [1] p. 5 (also [2] p. 9). A↑B↑C is (A↑B)↑C [2] p. 10. The DTSS-Win simulator, which runs a compiler of February 1965, gives (-2)↑3 = -8: the sign was already kept by then.
