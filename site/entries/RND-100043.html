    RND Function

    Rounds x at the digit of 10 to the power y.

    Layer
    FX700P_core — Casio FX-700P BASIC
    Dialects
    Casio FX-700P BASIC

    Syntax

    RND(x,y)

    In the manual (FX-700P Instruction Manual, p. 61): RND (x, y)

    Returns: Real number

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    x M numeric expression Value to round.
    y M numeric expression Power of 10 of the digit to keep: -2 rounds to hundredths.

    Equivalents in other dialects

    MATH_ROUND Round to a given digit

    No equivalent in: Dartmouth BASIC (1st edition), Dartmouth BASIC (2nd edition), Dartmouth BASIC (3rd edition), Dartmouth BASIC (4th edition), Dartmouth Card BASIC October 1964, Sinclair ZX80 BASIC 4K ROM
