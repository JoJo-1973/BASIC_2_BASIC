    EXP Function

    e raised to the argument.

    Layer
    DARTMOUTH_1ED_core — Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition
    Dialects
    Dartmouth BASIC (1st edition), Dartmouth BASIC (2nd edition), Dartmouth Card BASIC October 1964
    In Dartmouth BASIC (3rd edition)
    replaced by EXP of DARTMOUTH_3ED_layer
    In Dartmouth BASIC (4th edition)
    replaced by EXP of DARTMOUTH_3ED_layer

    Redefined in a later layer: EXP — A result too large gives a message.

    Syntax

    EXP(x)

    In the manual (BASIC Instruction Manual, May 1964 (First Draft), p. 5): EXP(A) differs from the rebuilt syntax

    Returns: Number, range [0, ∞) M

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    x M formula Any formula.
