    PRINT Statement

    Types the values of formulas and labels on a line.

    Layer
    DARTMOUTH_1ED_core — Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition
    Dialects
    Dartmouth BASIC (1st edition)

    replaced by PRINT of DARTMOUTH_2ED_layer

    replaced by PRINT of DARTMOUTH_CARD_layer

    Redefined in a later layer: PRINT — The semicolon packs the output: the next item starts at the next multiple of three characters.

    Syntax

    Commas separate the items. A label can be followed directly by a formula: PRINT "ROOTS =" S+T. PRINT alone skips a line.

    PRINT [<label | formula | label formula> [, <label | formula | label formula>]... [,]]

    In the manual (BASIC Instruction Manual, May 1964 (First Draft), p. 7-8): PRINT differs from the rebuilt syntax

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    label M label Text in quotes, typed as it is.
    formula M formula Any formula: its value is typed.

    Notes

    Five values fit on a line, in zones of 15 characters (from [2]); the May manual shows the rule only by examples. Whether a final comma keeps the next PRINT on the same line is stated only in [2].

    DTSS-Win (compiler of February 1965) prints the round brackets of a label as square ones: PRINT "()" and PRINT "[]" both print []. The cause (the internal code of the GE-235 or the simulator) is not known.

    • Width of the fixed tabulation zones: 15 columns to verify
    • Variable tab stops: No to verify
