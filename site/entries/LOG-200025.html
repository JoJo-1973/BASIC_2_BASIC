    LOG Function

    Natural logarithm.

    Layer
    DARTMOUTH_1ED_core — Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition
    Dialects
    Dartmouth BASIC (1st edition), Dartmouth BASIC (2nd edition), Dartmouth Card BASIC October 1964
    In Dartmouth BASIC (3rd edition)
    replaced by LOG of DARTMOUTH_3ED_layer
    In Dartmouth BASIC (4th edition)
    replaced by LOG of DARTMOUTH_3ED_layer

    Redefined in a later layer: LOG — A negative or zero argument gives a message.

    Syntax

    LOG(x)

    In the manual (BASIC Instruction Manual, May 1964 (First Draft), p. 5): LOG(A) differs from the rebuilt syntax

    Returns: Number
