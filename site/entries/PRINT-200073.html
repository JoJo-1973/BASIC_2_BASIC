    PRINT Statement

    Types the values of formulas and labels on a line.

    Layer
    DARTMOUTH_3ED_layer — Dartmouth BASIC, 3rd edition
    Dialects
    Dartmouth BASIC (3rd edition)
    In Dartmouth BASIC (4th edition)
    replaced by PRINT of DARTMOUTH_4ED_layer

    Redefines PRINT of the previous layer.
    Differences: Packed zones of 6, 9 or 12 characters.

    Redefined in a later layer: PRINT — Strings and TAB(n).

    Syntax

    ',' moves to the next zone of 15 characters (after the fifth, to the next line); ';' moves to the next multiple of three characters, at least six per number. A PRINT ending with a comma or semicolon does not end the line; PRINT alone skips a line.

    PRINT [<label | formula | label formula> [<, | ;> <label | formula | label formula>]... [, | ;]]

    In the manual (BASIC Language Reference Manual (June 1965, rev. January 1967), p. 23-24): PRINT differs from the rebuilt syntax

    ParameterTypeAllowed valuesDescription
    label M label Text in quotes, typed as it is; it fills whole zones.
    formula M formula Any formula: its value is typed.
