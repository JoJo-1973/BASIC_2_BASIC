    Open questions 9

    Part II of the May 1964 manual is missing: GOSUB, RETURN, DIM, REM, RND, INT and the arrays are taken from the October 1964 manual. Kurtz [9] answers part of it (the four statements are DIM, GOSUB, RETURN, REM; lists from 1 to 10 without DIM; INT truncates), quoting the complete manual of June 1964, which is still to be found.

    Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition

    Card BASIC (chapter IV of the October 1964 manual: PAGE, MAT, relations EQU LSS ...) is not entered: is it worth a dialect of its own?

    Dartmouth BASIC, 2nd edition

    The 3rd edition is built from the GE manual of January 1967 and from Wikipedia: check it against the Dartmouth 3rd edition manual (1 January 1966) if it can be found. SGN, the unary minus after the power and INT as floor are now confirmed by Kurtz [9].

    Dartmouth BASIC, 3rd edition

    DEF: can a function have more than one argument in the 4th edition? Maximum string length (strings over 60 characters need a DIM)? GOSUB and FOR nesting limits?

    Dartmouth BASIC, 4th edition

    GOSUB in the 1st edition: the October 1964 manual says a GOSUB inside a subroutine does not work, Kurtz [9] that GOSUB used a stack for the return address. Which one is right for 1964?

    Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition

    Errors: the May manual shows only five messages; the others come from the October 1964 manual. The simulator DTSS-Win runs a compiler of February 1965 that already has 3rd edition behaviours (nested GOSUB, OUT OF DATA, LOG OF NEGATIVE NUMBER, SQUARE ROOT OF NEGATIVE NUMBER, the sign of (-2)↑3), so it cannot confirm the 1964 behaviour. Why does it print 1 for 1/0?

    Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition

    FOR: the May manual wants a single letter, the October manual any unsubscripted variable (FOR X1): was the 1st edition really stricter? Does a loop whose start is past the end run zero times, as in the October manual?

    Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition

    Variables and program in memory, internal character code of the GE-235: to be taken from the compiler listings BA-1..BA-3 [7].

    Dartmouth BASIC, 1st edition

    DTSS-Win prints the round brackets of a label as square ones (PRINT "()" gives []): does the GE-235 code of a label have no round brackets, or is it the simulator?
